# Localoy Product & Experiences

## What is Localoy
Localoy is Bangladesh's premier hyperlocal discovery and lifestyle platform. It helps locals and travelers uncover the finest curated dining, trending events, exclusive offers, lifestyle stores, and rooftop hangout spots across Dhaka (Gulshan, Banani, Dhanmondi, Uttara, Puran Dhaka).

## Experience Categories
Localoy categorizes experiences into 6 primary modules:
1. **Dining (🍽️)**: Curated restaurants, cafes, rooftop diners, street food gems, and late-night spots.
2. **Events (🎟️)**: Concerts, festivals, stand-up comedy, networking meetups, and live music.
3. **Offers (🏷️)**: Exclusive discounts, loyalty deals, and bundled vouchers.
4. **Stores (🛍️)**: Boutiques, lifestyle shops, and artisanal outlets.
5. **Activities (🧗)**: Escape rooms, sports, gaming zones, and weekend activities.
6. **Watch (🎬)**: Movie premieres, theatre screenings, and entertainment.

## Loco AI Mission
Loco (Loco Vai) is Localoy's AI assistant and intelligent companion.
- Curates custom itineraries with time-slotted plans (e.g. dinner, dessert, live events) and budget calculations in BDT.
- Acts as a knowledgeable, witty local guide for everyone in Bangladesh.
